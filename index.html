About Me

I'm an Aerospace Engineering undergraduate student at Georgia Tech with a passion for learning through rapid prototyping and testing, especially in UAV and mechatronics fields.
I have a deep curiosity and a relentless pursuit for mastering skills across many fields so that no matter what dream I have, I know I can make it a reality.

In the makerspace and on my robotics team, I actively engage in teamwork and collaboration by sharing ideas, troubleshooting designs, and co-developing innovative prototypes alongside engineers of many different backgrounds. As a leader in these settings, I mentor others by teaching prototyping techniques, guiding them through the iterative design processes, and fostering a collaborative environment to drive project success.

I am seeking internship opportunities where I can leverage my prototyping skill set while further developing my design and simulation abilities in a dynamic environment that fosters creativity and encourages cutting-edge innovation.
