## Overview

For the SolidWorks unit of ME 1670, I modeled the Space Shuttle as interlocking components — the Solid Rocket Boosters, External Tank and orbiter — and printed them on a Stratasys SLS printer with tolerances designed for the process.

<Figure src="3-6-m.jpg" alt="NASA three-view technical drawing of the Space Shuttle orbiter with dimensions" caption="NASA three-view drawing used as the dimensional reference." contain />

## Design process

I worked from NASA's technical drawings to keep proportions accurate, turning them into SolidWorks sketches for each part. Then I picked the best places to add custom interlocks so the SRBs, tank and orbiter would connect cleanly.

<Figure src="img-5819.jpg" alt="SolidWorks model of the full Shuttle stack" caption="The assembled stack in SolidWorks." />

## Tolerancing & fabrication

The key challenge was designing for SLS. I respected minimum feature thicknesses and adjusted dimensions to keep thin features strong. Some features — like the SRB attachment points — took a few rounds of refinement because they started out too tight.

## Results

The printed model worked: all three components interlocked once the unsintered powder was cleared from the interlock voids. The SLS printer's detail made the hand-sized model turn out beautifully.
