## Overview

A personal exploration of RF modules and PCB fabrication: a remote that can selectively switch any device fitted with a matching receiver or MOSFET switch. Making the board myself mattered — as **Electronics Apprentice**, it was my job to create the operating procedure for the Invention Studio's new PCB mill.

## Circuit design

In KiCad I designed a microcontroller circuit that interfaces with an RF module and supports MOSFET switch control, exploring several RF libraries and protocols for reliable transmission.

## PCB fabrication

I fabricated the board on the studio's new mill and developed the procedure along the way — learning FlatCAM to turn KiCad output into G-code and iterating on milling parameters until traces came out clean and reliable.

<Video src="/media/custom-remote/pcb-mill.mp4" caption="Isolation-milling a board on the Invention Studio's PCB mill." size="prose" />

## Results

The enclosure, designed in SolidWorks and printed, was tailored to the compact PCB while keeping the controls and OLED accessible. The finished remote reliably triggered devices out to about 10 m.
