## Overview

I'm an undergraduate researcher in Georgia Tech's [Aerospace Robotics Lab](https://aerospaceroboticslab.github.io/), led by Dr. Yashwanth Nakka. My role is to design, build and test a group of small, autonomy-capable quadcopters that will work together to manipulate their environment. The lab uses them as testbeds for research in swarm coordination, mapping and localization.

## Motion capture

Along with the vehicles, I developed the flight code and procedures for flying them with the lab's motion capture system, which gives high-precision position and attitude for localization.
