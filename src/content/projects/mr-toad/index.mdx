## Overview

Mr Toad's Wild Rover is my third project funded by the Invention Studio's Maker Grant program. The goal: a durable, autonomy-capable rover in the spirit of the iRobot FirstLook — the small robot first responders throw into dangerous spaces to scout ahead.

<Figure src="irobot.jpg" alt="iRobot 110 FirstLook rover in rubble" caption="The iRobot 110 FirstLook that inspired the project." />

## Inspiration & goals

I found the FirstLook while researching harsh-environment rovers for my robotics team. It amazed me how versatile such a small device could be — compact enough to throw, durable enough to survive the landing, and carrying a full sensor and comms package. I'm also using the project to learn **ArduRover** for autonomous path planning and navigation.

Laying the rover out in SolidWorks was mostly straightforward — except for the motors. The ones I'd acquired forced an awkward placement that needed power transmitted through gears or rope. Both would have overcomplicated the system, so I changed motors instead.

The climbing stilts were the most interesting problem. Their presence meant I couldn't use a shaft to retain the front wheels, and the long shafts raise shear-stress concerns since the stilts must lift the rover's full weight.
