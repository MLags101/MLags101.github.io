Mr Toad's Wild Rover

Overview

Mr Toad's Wild Rover is the third project of mine funded by the Maker Grant program of the Invention Studio. The goal is to develop a durable, autonomous-capable rover similar to the iRobot FirstLook, used by first responders to scout dangerous environments.

Inspiration & Goals

I discovered the FirstLook while researching harsh-environment rovers for my robotics team. It amazed me how versatile such a small device could be — compact enough to be thrown by hand, durable enough to survive the landing, with a full array of sensors and communications. I'm also using the project to learn ArduRover for autonomous path planning and navigation.

Design Challenges

Creating the layout in SolidWorks was fairly straightforward, though due to the motor sizes I acquired, I had to pursue a peculiar motor placement requiring power transmission via gears or rope. The climbing stilts integration presented interesting challenges — their presence meant I could not use a shaft to retain the front wheels, and the long shaft lengths raise shear stress concerns since the stilts must lift the full rover weight.

Current Status

The project is currently waiting for stage approval from the Studio and revising the CAD to address the mentioned issues. I'm excited to tackle these challenges and get into hands-on prototyping!
