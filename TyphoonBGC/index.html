Typhoon Brushless Gimbal Controller

Overview

I started this project to address a need in small UAV prototyping — a compact, low-cost brushless motor controller designed specifically for camera gimbals. Existing solutions were either too expensive and oversized (ODrive, Moteus) or plagued with software issues (STorM32).

V1 — Teensy + L6234

I began by researching brushless control principles and studying the open-source schematics of the STorM32, ODrive, and Moteus. My first board tested the L6234 motor driver chip with a Teensy generating control signals. I designed the schematic following the datasheet and had the board fabricated by JLCPCB.

Testing & Setback

Assembling was difficult without a reflow oven — I hand-soldered each SMD component. I got promising initial results, but before I could proceed, I accidentally shorted my power supply cable through the microcontroller, destroying my only Teensy. I decided to take the success I had and move forward to a fully integrated design.

V2 — ATMega328 Integrated

My newest iteration integrates an ATMega328PD microcontroller directly on a double-sided PCB alongside the L6234, maximizing compactness. The schematic was informed by work from ElectroNoobs on YouTube for sensorless slow brushless control. I am currently awaiting the boards and excited to assemble and test.
