Space Shuttle Model

Overview

As part of the SolidWorks unit in my ME 1670 (Engineering Design) course, I designed a 3D model of the Space Shuttle with interlocking components: the Solid Rocket Boosters (SRBs), External Tank, and Shuttle orbiter. The parts were printed on a Stratasys SLS printer with precise engineering tolerances.

Design Process

I referenced technical drawings from NASA's website to ensure proportional accuracy, converting sketches into SolidWorks drawings for each part. I identified the best areas for adding custom interlocking mechanisms so the SRBs, External Tank, and Shuttle would connect seamlessly.

Tolerancing & Fabrication

A key challenge was incorporating engineering tolerances for the SLS printing process. I adhered to minimum feature thickness requirements, adjusting dimensions to maintain structural integrity. Some features, like the SRB attachment points, required iterative refinement as they were initially too tight.

Results

The final printed model was a success — all three components interlocked after cleaning the unsintered powder from the interlock voids. I was proud of how well everything fit, and credit the incredible detail of the SLS printer for how nice the hand-sized model turned out.
